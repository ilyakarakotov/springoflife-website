## Our story

Spring of Life Church began in October 1994, when about 30 Baptist immigrants from across the former Soviet Union started meeting for worship in Everett, in the building of Bethel Baptist Church. The church was officially registered in 1995.

From the first years there was a Sunday school for the kids, and a Russian school so children who had moved to America wouldn't lose their native language. The church grew quickly, with about 30 people baptized every year, and it moved twice more within Everett as it grew.

In 2005 the church bought a building of its own in Mukilteo. After a little over a year of renovation, it was dedicated on April 30, 2006, and it's still our home today. Pavel Belev has served as senior pastor since 2021, and we now worship in Russian at 10:00 am and in English at 12:00 pm.

"Thus far the Lord has helped us." (1 Samuel 7:12)
